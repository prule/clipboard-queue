Cross-platform notes
Windows / Linux keep the same window; the menu-bar popover becomes a system-tray menu, ⌘V reads as Ctrl+V, and the hotkeys shift to Ctrl+Alt+←/→. Paste interception needs a per-platform hook (NSEvent tap on macOS, low-level keyboard hook on Windows, X11/Wayland shim on Linux) behind one expect/actual interface.
